# 🏡 EstatePilot AI

### 🤖 Every Inquiry Becomes an Opportunity.

**EstatePilot AI** is an AI-powered customer operations agent designed for the real estate industry. It helps customers discover properties, get answers to their questions, request property visits, submit inquiries, and raise support tickets through an intelligent interface.

The project aims to automate customer interactions, improve lead management, and reduce missed inquiries through AI-powered assistance and workflow automation.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The real estate industry faces significant challenges in managing customer inquiries, responding to property-related questions, tracking potential buyers, scheduling property visits, and providing timely customer support.

Traditional customer service processes often rely on manual communication through phone calls, emails, and messaging platforms. This can lead to missed inquiries, delayed responses, forgotten follow-ups, inefficient lead management, and poor customer experiences.

Real estate businesses also struggle to manage multiple customer conversations simultaneously while maintaining accurate records and ensuring that complex issues receive appropriate human attention.

**EstatePilot AI aims to solve these challenges by providing an intelligent customer operations platform that automates routine interactions, simplifies property discovery, organizes customer leads, streamlines visit requests, and escalates unresolved issues to human representatives.**

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

EstatePilot AI combines AI-powered customer assistance, property discovery, lead management, and workflow automation to make real estate customer operations more efficient.

The platform is designed to help customers find suitable properties, receive relevant information, submit inquiries, request property visits, and raise support tickets. It also aims to help real estate businesses organize customer interactions, track requests, and reduce missed business opportunities.

The ultimate goal is to create a faster, smarter, and more convenient experience for both customers and real estate professionals.

## ✨ Features

* 🏠 **Smart Property Search:** Find properties based on location, budget, property type, and number of bedrooms.
* 🤖 **Intelligent Customer Support:** Helps answer customer questions about properties and real estate services.
* 📋 **Automated Lead Management:** Captures and organizes customer inquiries to help businesses manage potential buyers.
* 📅 **Property Visit Requests:** Enables customers to request property visits conveniently.
* 🎫 **Support Ticket Management:** Supports the creation and tracking of customer support requests.
* 👥 **Human Escalation:** Provides a way to route complex or unresolved customer issues to human representatives.

* 📱 **Responsive Interface:** Designed to provide a user-friendly experience across desktop and mobile devices.
* 🔔 **Status Tracking:** Helps track customer requests and support ticket statuses.
* 🔍 **Property Information:** Presents property details, prices, locations, and available amenities.
* 🔮 **Future Scalability:** Designed to accommodate additional integrations and real estate management features.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Customer Interaction:** A customer interacts with the EstatePilot AI platform.
2. **Requirement Understanding:** The system identifies the customer's property requirements or support needs.
3. **Property Discovery:** Customers can search available property data using relevant criteria.
4. **Inquiry Management:** Customers can submit inquiries and express interest in properties.
5. **Visit Requests:** Customers can request property visits.
6. **Support Management:** Customers can raise support tickets for assistance.
7. **Workflow Processing:** Supported actions can be organized and processed through the application's workflows.
8. **Human Escalation:** Unresolved issues can be directed to human representatives.
